# Marrow

**Marrow** is a clean, minimalist and fairly customisable **Android launcher**, focusing on eliminating colour overstimulation and distractions.

## Key Features & Tools

### Deep Customization
- **Theme Modes**: Switch between Light, Dark, and Extra Dark (pitch-black AMOLED).
- **Typography & Formatting**: Geist, Geist mono and Fira mono fonts available, with future updates to roll out more.
- **Glanceable Widgets**: Minimal home screen indicators for unread notifications, priority alerts, battery, screen time, and weather.
- **Custom Gestures**: Configurable swipe-up, down, left or right gestures for search, Notespace, or launching your favorite apps.

### Notespace & Smart Reminders
- **Distraction-Free Scratchboard**: Clean board to quickly capture notes and thoughts without unnecessary clutter.
- **Push Reminders**: Easily schedule notes with exact date and time triggers that send actionable heads-up notifications.
- **Intuitive Interactions**: Drag and drop to reorder notes, and tap the circle completion button to dismiss reminders with a snappy overshoot animation.
- **Quick Gesture Access**: Bind Notespace to any launcher gesture (e.g., swipe down) for instant access.

### Priority Alerts
- **Hardware & Edge Alerts**: Never miss important notifications with customisable edge glow and torch flashes.
- **Priority Widget**: Dedicated yellow priority indicator on your home screen displaying unread urgent alerts.

## ! Beta !
**Marrow** is currently under development, with more features rolling out for full release. The launcher is **NOT** expected to be available through the google play store any time in the near future, with updates available within the launcher itself.
